DesignSafe-CI featured our research on aerodynamic mitigators designed to help solar trackers withstand strong winds. Working with researchers from Colorado State University and the University at Buffalo, we tested the devices on a six-row scale model at the NSF NHERI Wall of Wind facility at Florida International University.

The tests showed that the devices increased the critical wind speed at which the tracker system becomes unstable by about 25%. [Read the full DesignSafe-CI article](https://designsafe-ci.org/community/news/2026/september/new-aerodynamic-devices-prevent-wind-damage-solar-panels-wall-of-wind/) for more about the experiments and their implications for solar energy resilience.
